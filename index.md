I am a Senior Researcher at the Indian Institute of Science ([IISc](https://www.iisc.ac.in/){:target="_blank"}), Bengaluru, where my work focuses on computational science, parameter
estimation, and inverse modelling methods for subsurface hydrology. I work with [Sekhar Muddu](https://civil.iisc.ac.in/~muddu/){:target="_blank"} on variety of subsurface hydrology modelling topics. I am currently working on continental-scale subsurface parameter estimation using billions of inverse simulations with physics-based models, implemented on parallel computing architectures and executed on the [PARAM Pravega](https://www.serc.iisc.ac.in/supercomputer/for-traditional-hpc-simulations-param-pravega/){:target="_blank"} supercomputer.


I am also a Senior Engineer in the UK Water Management team at [AtkinsRéalis](https://www.atkinsrealis.com/){:target="_blank"}, where I lead the development of automation solutions and computational tools for post-processing, model diagnostics, and anomaly detection in [UK Environment Agency’s](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/environment-agency){:target="_blank"} numerical groundwater models. I work closely with [Simon Wood](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simon-wood-8a666838/){:target="_blank"}, [Lesley McWilliam](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lesley-mcwilliam-03a062216/){:target="_blank"}, [Helen Stockham](https://www.linkedin.com/in/helen-stockham-561392b3/){:target="_blank"} and [Vicky Fry](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vicky-fry-964a96416/){:target="_blank"} on these modelling and automation initiatives. In addition, I conduct numerical modelling and scenario modelling to investigate the response of UK groundwater systems to changes in subsurface abstraction and climate forcings.

Prior to this, I led a team at [FluxGen](https://fluxgen.com/){:target="_blank"} in developing numerical groundwater models using [MODFLOW](https://www.usgs.gov/software/modflow-6-usgs-modular-hydrologic-model){:target="_blank"} to quantify artificial recharge through lakes, assess groundwater budgets, and understand aquifer recharge dynamics resulting from lake recharge. The work included developing and delivering a scalable numerical groundwater recharge model for [Adani Green Energy](https://www.adanigreenenergy.com/){:target="_blank"}, working with [Ganesh Shankar](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ganesh-shankar/){:target="_blank"} and [Bhargav Roy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/roybhargav/){:target="_blank"}. I also worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at the intersection of hydrology and machine learning at [SmartTerra](https://www.smartterra.io/){:target="_blank"} with [Gokul Krishna](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gokulgovindu/){:target="_blank"} and [Navaneethan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/navaneethans/){:target="_blank"}. My work focused on building hybrid/surrogate machine learning and physics-based numerical models for accelerated flood predictions, as well as developing deep learning models to classify wetland vegetation in spectrally complex environments using hyperspectral satellite imagery.
